WeReadLyrics adapts the rounded rectangle SDF and refraction shaders from
backdrop/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/kyant/backdrop/internal/Shaders.kt
and the glass capsule, highlight and spring interaction design of
app/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/kyant/backdrop/catalog/components/LiquidBottomTabs.kt.
The native motion adaptation also follows the spring parameters and
pressure/highlight behavior of catalog/utils/DampedDragAnimation.kt and
catalog/utils/InteractiveHighlight.kt. WeReadLyrics adds a front selection
lens sampling an offscreen accent-colored tab row and removes ripple feedback.
The integration uses native Android Views, RenderNode and RenderEffect;
it does not include the Compose catalog or require the source checkout at build time.
